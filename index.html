    How to play
    
    
      
  • You drive at 90 km/h toward a song playing 1.4 km away. The music kicks in after 2 seconds on the road, and the drive takes under a minute.
    • 
      
  • Far away you mostly hear the bass and the echo. Up close the melody comes through.
    • 
      
  • Buzz the moment you recognize it. The distance freezes while you type.
    • 
      
  • The music stops while you guess. A wrong guess moves you 150 m closer. You get 3 buzzes.
    • 
      
  • Wind gusts come and go, so listen for the gaps.
